# Data sources and attribution

## O*NET

This site includes information from the O*NET Database by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA). Used under the CC BY 4.0 license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). O*NET® is a trademark of USDOL/ETA. OpenPath has modified all or some of this information. USDOL/ETA has not approved, endorsed or tested these modifications.

- Occupations, related (alternate) job titles, task statements and technology skills: O*NET 30 release.
- Skills, education levels, interest profiles and job zones: earlier O*NET releases. Where a newer occupation has no published survey data yet, OpenPath uses the most similar occupation and labels it as an estimate.

Source: https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html

## Pay

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS).

- National pay percentiles and employment: May 2025 where published, otherwise May 2024.
- State median pay: May 2024.

Source: https://www.bls.gov/oes/

## College programs

## Role-specific profiles

Researched in October 2026 from BLS, professional associations and credential bodies. Each researched pay range links to its source. The Astronaut profile uses NASA's published requirements (https://nasa.gov/humans-in-space/astronauts/become-an-astronaut) and pay reported by Fortune (September 2025).
