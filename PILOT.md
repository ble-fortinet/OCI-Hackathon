# OpenPath pilot: one class period

Goal: get a real usage number for the submission ("X of Y students found a career they hadn't heard of").

## Run it (about 15 minutes)

1. Share the link: https://hackathonvanguard.vercel.app
2. Each student does **Quick match** (about 2 minutes) and opens their top match.
3. Each student answers three questions (show of hands, a shared form, or a quick message):
   1. Had you heard of your top match, or any of your other five matches, before today? (yes / no)
   2. Is there at least one career you now want to look into? (yes / no)
   3. Could you explain why your top match appeared? (yes / partly / no)

Don't collect names. You only need the counts.
